Lesson 017: Scientific notation and powers of ten

Learning goal: Translate positive quantities between decimal notation and normalized scientific notation, and verify that the represented value and units are unchanged.

Prerequisites: Lessons 005-016: multiplication, division, decimals, units, variables and ratios. Time: 15-30 minutes, plus optional reading of 5 minutes. Python 3; no additional packages required.

2. Powers of ten, taught from the beginning

An exponent counts repeated multiplication: 10^3 means 101010 = 1000. In this lesson the base is always 10. We define 10^0 = 1. A negative exponent means a reciprocal: 10^(-1)=1/10=0.1 and 10^(-3)=1/1000=0.001. A negative exponent does not make the number negative.

Multiplying by 10 moves the decimal point one place to the right; dividing by 10 moves it one place to the left. Scientific notation writes a positive number as a times 10^n, where the coefficient a is at least 1 and less than 10, and n is an integer (whole number, possibly negative or zero). This is called normalized scientific notation. The powers supply the scale; the coefficient supplies the remaining value. Zero is a separate case and is outside today’s normalization tasks.

In Python use ** for powers: 103 and 10(-3). The ^ operator is not exponentiation in Python. A compact number literal such as 2.4e3 means 2.4*10**3. Here e is part of numeric notation, not a variable or the exponential function. 2.4e-3 means 2.4*10**(-3). The unit still belongs in the variable name or written label.

3. Separate worked examples

A fictional cable is 7200 mm long. Its normalized form is 7.2 times 10^3 mm: move the decimal three places left in the coefficient, then compensate by multiplying by 1000. Python: cable_mm = 7.2 * 10**3, or cable_mm = 7.2e3. Both represent 7200 mm; the unit was not converted.

A fictional thin sheet is 0.0046 m thick. Its normalized form is 4.6 times 10^(-3) m. Python: sheet_m = 4.6e-3. The coefficient was enlarged by 1000, so the power compensates by dividing by 1000. Check by expanding the power.

72 times 10^2 also equals 7200, but it is not normalized because its coefficient is at least 10. Scientific notation changes representation. A unit conversion changes the numerical value and unit together while preserving the physical quantity. Do not confuse the two operations.

4. Assigned practice: an aerospace data card

These supplied quantities are fictional educational inputs, not measurements or a physical simulation. Keep their units unchanged.

A. Test-track length: 36000 m. B. Optical-window thickness: 0.00082 m. C. Sensor sampling interval: 0.000025 s. D. Test-chamber pressure: 125000 Pa. Pa is the pressure unit pascal; no pressure physics is needed. E. Reference speed: 8 m/s.

  5. A colleague writes B as 8.2e3 m and C as 25e-6 s. Explain separately whether each has the supplied value and whether each is normalized. Correct the representation where needed.
  6. Explain why a smaller positive value can have a larger coefficient in normalized notation. Use B and C; compare both coefficient and power.

5. Checks, interpretation and optional stretch

Success checks: each positive coefficient is at least 1 and less than 10; expanding the power reconstructs the decimal value; the unit is unchanged; e notation and coefficient-times-power agree; exponent signs fit the size of the numbers. A negative exponent must not create a negative physical quantity. Restart the kernel and run completed cells top-to-bottom.

Write one sentence explaining what scientific notation helps you see, and one explaining what it cannot tell you about the reliability of these fictional data. More displayed digits do not establish more measurement accuracy.

Optional stretch, with taught arithmetic only: 1000 m = 1 km. Convert A to km, then express the result in normalized scientific notation. Explain why the exponent can change even though the physical length has not changed.

Remediation: if exponent signs are confusing, start with 10, 1, 0.1 and 0.01 and expand each as multiplication or division. If a coefficient is outside the required range, shift it one decimal place and compensate with one factor of 10. Record comfortable / needs practice for powers, normalization, e notation and units.
